2 · Test details

Colour brightness · not matched (standard observer)

Use a participant code, not a name. Red–green: measure the pupil in the test lighting; the cylinder's size scales with the square of any pupil error. Colour-mix: no pupil needed; screen at 25–35 cm (30 cm best). The axis never depends on the pupil.

Before you start

  1. Glasses and contact lenses off (or on, to measure the astigmatism left over them: record which in Notes). No coloured filters.
  2. Cover the eye not being tested. Keep the head upright and at 50 cm from the screen (colour-mix needs 25–35 cm).
  3. Two patches of lines appear. Look at each in turn, as long as you like, and answer which side's lines look sharper (ignore which colour looks brighter).
  4. Answer with ← / → keys, or tap the left or right side of the screen. If they look the same, press ↓ or tap the middle ("Same").
  5. Three practice answers come first. Press Esc or "Stop" to abandon.
